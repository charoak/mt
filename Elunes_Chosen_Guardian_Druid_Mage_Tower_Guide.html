01 · Encounter Overview

Objective

Complete the solo tank challenge The Highlord's Return. Defeat Variss, then Kruul, while keeping Prophet Velen and Kor'vas Bloodthorn alive. Guardian completion awards the Fel Werebear form; completing a Mage Tower challenge also contributes the applicable class transmog reward.

02 · Talents & Stat Priority

Core picks

  • Galactic Guardian: ranged pressure and eye kills.
  • Twin Moonfire: improves distributed lunar damage.
  • Elune's Favored: passive healing from Arcane damage, if currently available.
  • Incapacitating Roar: stops Nether Storm.
  • Wild Charge: recovery tool after knockbacks.

Useful flex tools

  • Renewal for an extra emergency heal.
  • Typhoon to create space or interrupt susceptible adds.
  • Improved Barkskin / survival nodes over minor open-world utility.
  • Incarnation for the Kruul burn.

Stats

Versatility → Haste ≈ Critical Strike ≈ Mastery

The encounter source places Versatility first while describing the remaining stats as close. Favor useful sockets and working enchants over tiny secondary differences.

03 · Gear, Legacy Items & Consumables

Gear rules

  • Gear is scaled; raw current item level is not the whole answer.
  • Use equipped items with sockets and enchants that remain active at the scaled level.
  • Prefer one offensive trinket plus one reliable damage or defensive option.
  • Do not depend on tier, legendary, Azerite, Covenant, or other disabled bonus text.
  • Test every special effect inside the instance before farming around it.

Legacy optimization

  • Legacy socket gear can offer extra secondary stats after scaling.
  • Old enchants may work only within item-level restrictions.
  • Legacy farming is optional; execution has much greater value.
  • Avoid old items whose proc or set bonus becomes inactive.

04 · Phase 1 — Inquisitor Variss

  1. Fight near the center. Leave room behind you and keep infernal knockback lanes visible.
  2. Damage Variss from range. Moonfire is your safe, repeatable tool. Entering his Aura of Decay reduces maximum health; step in only with a reason and leave before stacks become dangerous.
  3. Interrupt Drain Life. A completed cast restores major boss health and erases progress. Move in for Skull Bash or use an available Holy Ward if necessary.
  4. Face Tormenting Eyes during Inquisitive Stare. Moonfire kills them because they resist physical damage. Do not turn away during the channel.
  5. Pick up Nether Horrors immediately. Moonfire each one as they approach Velen, group them, then use Incapacitating Roar shortly after Nether Storm begins to lock the cast and buy kill time.
  6. Sidestep Smoldering Infernal Smash. Never stand with your back to the platform edge. Infernals revive, so do not make them the main damage target.
  7. Preserve Holy Wards when stable. Each orb is a full heal plus mass disorient and is more valuable during Kruul.
  8. Transition cleanly. Before Variss dies, center yourself, stabilize health, clear urgent adds, and make sure Skull Bash is available.
Do not tunnel Variss. An eye stare, Drain Life, or infernal cone can end an otherwise clean attempt instantly.

05 · Phase 2 — Highlord Kruul

  1. Establish position in the center. Place Kruul so Netherstomp pools do not cut off safe space.
  2. Begin the burn. Use Incarnation, offensive trinket, potion, and drums only after you have control and confirmed each item works.
  3. Interrupt Twisted Reflection every time. If it lands, Kruul heals when he attacks you; a Holy Ward can remove the debuff in an emergency.
  4. Move immediately from Netherstomp. Keep puddles compact and away from the middle.
  5. Plan for Annihilate. Each cast makes the next more dangerous. Use a defensive before the hit and weave Holy Wards to disorient Kruul, buying extra time between casts.
  6. Continue Phase 1 mechanics. Horrors, infernals, eyes, and beams remain relevant. Kill or control urgent adds without abandoning Kruul's interrupt.
  7. Commit to damage. Kruul is a soft enrage: once Annihilate outscales your defensives, the attempt ends.

Annihilate 1

Ironfur and healthy HP; add Barkskin if the attempt is unstable.

Annihilate 2

Barkskin plus Ironfur; Frenzied Regeneration after the hit.

Annihilate 3+

Survival Instincts / strongest remaining coverage. Use Holy Wards to delay casts and extend the burn.

Exact lethal thresholds vary with tuning, gear, and active effects. Treat this as a planning ladder, not a guaranteed damage table.

07 · Defensive & Damage Cooldown Plans

Defensive ladder

  • Ironfur: maintain for physical contact and Annihilate preparation.
  • Barkskin: horror pickups, unstable overlaps, or early Annihilate.
  • Frenzied Regeneration: recover after meaningful damage.
  • Survival Instincts: late Annihilate or a dangerous overlap.
  • Renewal / health potion: independent emergency recovery.
  • Holy Ward: full heal, mass control, debuff recovery, or Annihilate delay.

Damage plan

  • Use Moonfire aggressively on eyes and spread targets.
  • Keep Thrash active when grouped in Bear Form.
  • Use Mangle on priority targets when safe.
  • Reserve Incarnation and consumable burst for Kruul.
  • Never sacrifice a required interrupt for an extra global.
  • Convert safe Rage into damage only after defensive needs are covered.

08 · Guardian-Specific Techniques

Lunar reach

Moonfire lets Guardian damage Variss outside Aura of Decay, tag horrors before they reach Velen, and kill physical-immune eyes.

Charge recovery

Wild Charge or Skull Bash can pull you back toward a valid target after a knockback. It is a recovery option, not permission to ignore Smash.

Form discipline

Use any ranged/caster-form tactic only while safe. Return to Bear Form before horrors or Kruul make physical contact.

Camera discipline

Zoom out and rotate the camera so infernals, eyes, portals, Velen, and platform edges remain visible.

Orb discipline

Mark Velen if needed and route around Holy Wards. Avoid consuming one by accident while kiting.

Control discipline

Roar after Nether Storm starts so the cast goes on cooldown; roaring too early wastes much of the control window.

10 · Final Preparation Checklist

Before entering

  • Repair gear and clear bags.
  • Bind Skull Bash, Incapacitating Roar, Wild Charge, Barkskin, Survival Instincts, Frenzied Regeneration, Renewal, and health potion.
  • Install or configure boss cast and debuff visibility.
  • Use Versatility-oriented gems/food as the safe baseline.
  • Set camera distance and nameplates.
  • Review the Annihilate defensive ladder.

After entering

  • Confirm Hero Talents and every special gear effect.
  • Confirm flask, food, potion, drums, weapon buff, and trinkets remain usable.
  • Check that tier/set text is active before counting on it.
  • Mark Velen if he is hard to track.
  • Place health potion and Holy Ward awareness near center screen.
  • Run one mechanics-only attempt before optimizing damage.
Ready check: If you can reliably stop both boss heals, control every horror wave, and avoid every Smash, you are ready to solve the final damage-and-defensive check.
